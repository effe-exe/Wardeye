# Governance

RiftEye is a maintainer-led project.

- **Maintainer:** Federico Vietti ([@effe-exe](https://github.com/effe-exe)). The Maintainer sets direction, has final say on what is merged and released, and owns the name, the official builds and the official catalogue and model releases.
- **Decisions** are discussed in issues and recorded in [docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md). Anyone can propose a change to a recorded decision by opening an issue.
- **Reviewers and committers** are invited from regular contributors as the project grows. Commit access does not change copyright or licensing: everyone's contributions come in under the [CLA](CLA.md).

## Licensing, stated plainly

- The code is AGPL-3.0-only ([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)). The project is free for everyone, with no commercial licences, paid tiers or paid services ([D-022](docs/decisions.md#d-022-free-for-everyone-closed-weights-a-showcase-for-gradeon)).
- The trained model weights are not published.
- Under the CLA, every contribution remains available under the AGPL-3.0, whatever else happens.

## Integrity commitments

- RiftEye never processes hidden information: hand cams, face-down cards, deck contents.
- RiftEye never redistributes broadcast footage or card artwork.
- Model changes are justified by measurements on real data, published on the leaderboard.
