The Wardeye **code** is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The Wardeye **name and logo** are not covered by that licence. They identify the official project and its official builds, so that people know what they are installing. This policy explains what you may do with them.

"Wardeye" and the Wardeye logo are trademarks of Federico Vietti (the "Maintainer"). This policy may be updated, and the latest version in this repository applies.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to the project truthfully.** For example: "works with Wardeye", "a plugin for Wardeye", "based on Wardeye", "a fork of Wardeye".
- **Redistribute unmodified official releases** under the Wardeye name.
- **Write about the project**: articles, videos, streams, talks and tutorials, including monetised content.
- **Use the name for non-commercial community activity**: meetups, Discord servers and user groups, as long as it is clear they are not run by the project.

## You need written permission to

- **Name a product, fork, extension-store listing, domain, app or company "Wardeye"**, or anything confusingly similar ("Wardeyes", "Ward Eye Pro", "Wardeye+" and so on).
- **Publish modified builds under the Wardeye name or logo**, including in the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons, Twitch Extensions or any app store. Forks are welcome: rename them and replace the logo. You can still say "a fork of Wardeye".
- **Use the logo** for anything other than linking to the official project.
- **Imply endorsement**, certification or partnership that does not exist.
- **Use the name in paid products or services**, for example a hosted "Wardeye" service or paid "Wardeye" overlays.

## Asking for permission

Open an issue with the `trademark` label describing what you want to do. For private matters, contact the Maintainer through the GitHub profile [@effe-exe](https://github.com/effe-exe).
