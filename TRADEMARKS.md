"RiftEye" and the RiftEye logo are trademarks of Federico Vietti (the "Maintainer"). This policy may be updated, and the latest version in this repository applies.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to the project truthfully.** For example: "works with RiftEye", "a plugin for RiftEye", "based on RiftEye", "a fork of RiftEye".
- **Redistribute unmodified official releases** under the RiftEye name.
- **Write about the project**: articles, videos, streams, talks and tutorials, including monetised content.
- **Use the name for non-commercial community activity**: meetups, Discord servers and user groups, as long as it is clear they are not run by the project.

## You need written permission to

- **Name a product, fork, extension-store listing, domain, app or company "RiftEye"**, or anything confusingly similar ("RiftEyes", "Rift Eye Pro", "RiftEye+" and so on).
- **Publish modified builds under the RiftEye name or logo**, including in the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons, Twitch Extensions or any app store. Forks are welcome: rename them and replace the logo. You can still say "a fork of RiftEye".
- **Use the logo** for anything other than linking to the official project.
- **Imply endorsement**, certification or partnership that does not exist.
- **Use the name in paid products or services**, for example a hosted "RiftEye" service or paid "RiftEye" overlays.

## Asking for permission

Open an issue with the `trademark` label describing what you want to do. For private matters, contact the Maintainer through the GitHub profile [@effe-exe](https://github.com/effe-exe).
