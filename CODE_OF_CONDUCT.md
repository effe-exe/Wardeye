# Code of conduct

Wardeye is a community of players, streamers, organisers and developers. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their level of experience or background.

**Expected:**

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect the players who appear in broadcasts. Do not post clips or screenshots of them, and never try to identify or expose private information.

**Not tolerated:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised content, doxxing, and sustained disruption, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

**Reporting:** contact the Maintainer privately ([@effe-exe](https://github.com/effe-exe)). Reports are handled confidentially.

**Consequences:** the Maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban participants who break these rules.
